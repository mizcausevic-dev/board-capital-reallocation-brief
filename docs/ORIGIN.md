# Origin

Board Capital Reallocation Brief exists after the scorecards, trust-room, procurement-answer, RFP assembly, proof-gap, board-memo, benchmark, scenario, allocation, and priority layers.

The earlier tools show evidence, narrative, benchmark, allocation posture, and board priority. This repo turns that stack into one capital-rotation surface so leadership can explain what to trim, what to protect, what to hold, where to redeploy, and which next move survives a board or diligence room.
